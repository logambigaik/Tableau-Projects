# 📊 HR Analytics Dashboard - Tableau Visualization

This project presents an **HR Analytics Dashboard** built using Tableau. It analyzes employee data to uncover trends in attrition, satisfaction, performance, and workforce composition.

🔗 **Live Dashboard:**  
👉 [View it on Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/views/HR_Data_Analytic_17520615340030/HRANALYTICSDASHBOARD?:language=en-GB&:sid=&:redirect=auth&:display_count=n&:origin=viz_share_link)

---

## 🧠 Project Highlights

- **Dataset:** HR Employee Data  
- **Tool Used:** [Tableau Public](https://public.tableau.com/)
- **Topics Covered:**
  - Attrition and retention rates
  - Job satisfaction and performance metrics
  - Departmental breakdown
  - Demographic and employment overview

## 🧩 Dashboard Panel Breakdown

### 1. **Attrition Overview**
- **Purpose:** Shows how many employees have left vs. stayed.
- **Insights:** Identifies attrition rates and trends over time.
- **Visualization:** KPIs and bar chart.

### 2. **Job Role vs. Attrition**
- **Purpose:** Compares attrition across different job roles.
- **Insights:** Highlights roles with high turnover.
- **Visualization:** Horizontal bar chart.

### 3. **Monthly Income & Job Satisfaction**
- **Purpose:** Correlates income levels with job satisfaction.
- **Insights:** Determines if income affects satisfaction or attrition.
- **Visualization:** Box plot or bubble chart.

### 4. **Education Field & Performance Rating**
- **Purpose:** Shows performance distribution across educational backgrounds.
- **Insights:** Evaluates if education relates to higher performance.
- **Visualization:** Grouped bar chart.

### 5. **Demographics Panel**
- **Purpose:** Displays gender, age, and marital status breakdown.
- **Insights:** Demographic trends in hiring and retention.
- **Visualization:** Pie charts and histograms.

### 6. **Filters Panel**
- **Purpose:** Filter by:
  - Job role
  - Department
  - Education
  - Gender
  - Marital Status
- **Insights:** Interactive filtering for deeper analysis.
